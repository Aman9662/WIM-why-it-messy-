The Mission

Why It's Messy was born out of frustration. Digital tools are fragmented, expensive, and filled with dark patterns. We are fixing that.

1. Radically Free

No paywalls, no subscriptions, no accounts. Tools should be accessible to everyone, especially students.

2. Privacy First

Your files are processed securely. Transfers expire in 10 minutes. We don't mine your data.

3. Beautiful UX

Functionality shouldn't mean ugly. We believe in design that brings pleasure to everyday tasks.

Powered by Modern Tech
