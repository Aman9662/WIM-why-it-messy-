Open Source Project

Built to Fight
Digital Deception

FakeDetector is a free, open-source platform that uses Google Gemini AI to detect fake content in all its forms — from AI-generated text and plagiarized ideas to fake news, scam jobs, phishing emails, and more.

In an era where AI-generated content is indistinguishable from human writing, and misinformation spreads faster than truth, we believe everyone deserves access to powerful, accurate detection tools — for free, forever.
